本隱私權政策說明 echohole（以下稱「我們」）開發的行動遊戲 BestSeller - Back To The Top（以下稱「本遊戲」）如何處理您的資訊。 我們的核心原則是：本遊戲盡可能少地蒐集資料，且不會蒐集能直接辨識您個人身分的資料。 下載、安裝或使用本遊戲，即表示您同意本政策所述之作法。

This Privacy Policy explains how echohole (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles your information in the mobile game BestSeller - Back To The Top (the “Game”). Our guiding principle is simple: the Game collects as little data as possible and does not collect information that directly identifies you. By downloading, installing, or using the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. 我們蒐集的資料1. Information We Collect

a) 儲存在您裝置上的遊戲進度
本遊戲會將您的遊戲進度與設定儲存在您裝置本機，包含關卡進度、金幣、升級、 圖鑑收集、成就狀態與語言／音量等設定。這些資料存放於您的裝置內，不會傳送給我們， 並會在您解除安裝本遊戲時一併移除。

b) Google Play 遊戲服務（成就 / 登入）
本遊戲整合 Google Play 遊戲服務，用於解鎖與同步成就。若您選擇登入，Google 會處理您的 Play 遊戲帳號資訊（例如玩家 ID、玩家代稱）以提供該服務。此互動由 Google 依其自身隱私權 政策管理；登入為選用，本遊戲離線亦可完整遊玩。

c) 廣告（Google AdMob）
本遊戲可能會顯示獎勵式廣告（例如觀看廣告以復活或獲得額外金幣）， 由 Google AdMob 提供。廣告服務可能會蒐集與使用裝置與廣告相關的識別碼（例如廣告 ID）、 概略的裝置資訊及互動資料，用於投放與衡量廣告。此類資料由 Google 蒐集與處理。

我們不會蒐集您的姓名、電子郵件、電話、精確定位、通訊錄或相片。

a) Game progress stored on your device
The Game saves your progress and settings locally on your device — including level progress, coins, upgrades, bestiary entries, achievement status, and preferences such as language and volume. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

b) Google Play Games Services (achievements / sign-in)
The Game integrates Google Play Games Services to unlock and sync achievements. If you choose to sign in, Google processes your Play Games account information (such as your player ID and gamer tag) to provide this service. This interaction is governed by Google’s own privacy policy. Sign-in is optional — the Game is fully playable offline.

c) Advertising (Google AdMob)
The Game may show rewarded ads (for example, watch an ad to revive or to earn extra coins), served by Google AdMob. Ad services may collect and use device and advertising identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), coarse device information, and interaction data to deliver and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by Google.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos.

  • 儲存並還原您的遊戲進度與設定；
  • 透過 Google Play 遊戲服務提供成就與（選用的）登入功能；
  • 顯示廣告並衡量其成效（若您使用廣告功能）。

我們不會販售您的資料。

  • To save and restore your game progress and settings;
  • To provide achievements and optional sign-in via Google Play Games Services;
  • To display and measure ads (if you use ad features).

We do not sell your data.

5. 您的選擇與控制5. Your Choices & Controls

  • 您可在遊戲的「設定」中清除遊戲資料，以刪除裝置上的本機進度。
  • 您可在裝置的系統設定中重設或限制廣告 ID（Android：設定 → Google → 廣告）。
  • 登入 Google Play 遊戲服務為選用；您可選擇不登入而繼續遊玩。
  • 解除安裝本遊戲會移除所有儲存在裝置上的本機資料。
  • You can clear game data in the Game’s Settings to delete local progress on your device.
  • You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Signing in to Google Play Games Services is optional; you may play without it.
  • Uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored data from your device.
